# shortsmith

shortsmith is a private, single-user tool built and operated by Jai Rajpal. It produces short educational videos for the operator's own YouTube channel, [Made How](https://www.youtube.com/@madehowshorts), and uploads them to that channel.

## What it does

- Generates original short-form explainer videos (script, voiceover, visuals, captions) with the help of AI tools. AI-generated content is labeled using YouTube's altered or synthetic content disclosure.
- Uploads those videos to the operator's own YouTube channel(s) on a schedule, using the YouTube Data API.
- Reads view and retention analytics for the operator's own videos to decide which topics to produce next.

## Who can use it

shortsmith is not a public app or service. It runs only on the operator's own machines and cloud accounts, and the only Google account authorized to use it is the operator's own. It does not access, collect, or display any other user's data, and it does not display YouTube content outside of YouTube.
